Privacy Policy

This is a free study site. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, and it does not sell or share personal information. Here is exactly what does happen when you visit.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who runs this site

The site is run by Sphe Hlongwa (GitHub: sphe-hlongwa). For any privacy question or request, contact me through my GitHub profile.

Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records the page you viewed, the referring page, your browser and screen size, and your country. Your IP address is not stored in a way that identifies you. I use these numbers only to see which pages are useful.

Data stored in your browser

The mock papers save a timer deadline in your browser’s local storage (key info2001a-mock-…-deadline) so the timer survives a page refresh. It stays on your device, is never sent to me, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.

What I do not collect

  • No names, email addresses or accounts.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Nothing you type into the on-page topic search leaves your browser.

Links to other sites

The site links to Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub and other external sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and I am not responsible for them.

Your rights

You can ask what, if anything, I hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, as provided by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws such as the GDPR. Contact me through GitHub as above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
